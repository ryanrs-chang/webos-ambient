Weather demos

Standalone pages, no build, no network. 1920x1080 scaled to the window. Switch weather with the buttons or keys 1-8; H hides the UI; ?k=rain picks one.

v1 CinematicFull-screen layered canvas: depth rain with bottom splashes, drifting multi-layer clouds, fog bands, gusting snow, stars + shooting star, soft sun rays, lightning. v2 Animated iconsBig animated Meteocons icon (MIT, vendored SVG) beside a big clock on a near-black background; the lightest option. v3 WindowRain-on-glass: refracting drops that trickle and wipe trails over dim city bokeh; frost for snow, mist for fog. v4 LineworkThin line-art weather under a sky arc where the sun or moon sits at the real hour; very few lit pixels.
Research, inspirations and licenses: SOURCES.md. Add ?h=14 to v4 to preview another hour.
